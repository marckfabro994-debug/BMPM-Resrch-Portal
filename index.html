Basura Mo Points Mo (BMPM)

An innovative campus waste management and incentive system empowering students of Gabaldon Vocational Agriculture High School toward sustainable environmental action.

Welcome to the BMPM Research Platform

This digital platform connects student participants with school environmental coordinators in real-time. By documenting and submitting recyclable waste materials, students earn redeemable ecological points while contributing vital data to our senior high school research study.

Live Cloud Sync

Submissions sent from student devices are instantly recorded in our secure cloud database, allowing immediate verification by admin researchers across different devices.

Environmental Impact

Promoting active recycling culture within Gabaldon Vocational Agriculture High School through measurable green points and accountable student portfolios.

Dedicated Admin Portal

Researchers and administrators have a dedicated dashboard to review submissions, verify waste types, and award points instantly.

Research Methodology

How the Sampling Researcher Works

Follow these simple steps to participate in the Basura Mo Points Mo (BMPM) study and earn your rewards.

STEP 01

Waste Collection & Sorting

Collect recyclable waste materials around the campus or home (e.g., Plastic Bottles, Paper & Cardboard, Metal Cans, or Biodegradables). Clean and bundle them properly.

STEP 02

Digital Submission

Go to the Submit Waste tab on your device, fill out your complete name, grade & section, waste category, weight/quantity, and attach photo proof or description. Click submit to record it instantly in the cloud database.

STEP 03

Verification & Points Awarding

The research admin on Device 2 reviews your submitted record in real-time. Once inspected and verified, points are credited to your student portfolio which you can check anytime!
